Master Resume

Turn your existing resume into a master resume. You review everything before it is saved.

Import a PDF or DOCX

You already have a master resume. An import proposes replacing the whole thing, and new entries get new IDs. To add a few items, use Add Info instead.
Importing uses the extractor model, and ANTHROPIC_API_KEY is not set. Export it and restart the app.

What happens

  1. Text is extracted from the file. Scanned or image-only files cannot be read (no OCR).
  2. Lines that look like an NRIC, date of birth, marital status or home address are removed before anything is stored or sent (P6).
  3. The extractor model maps the text to the master resume format, copying wording as written.
  4. Code then checks that every name, title, bullet and number appears in your file, and lists anything that does not.
  5. You review the result, fix anything in the patch editor, and apply it.
